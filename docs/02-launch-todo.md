## Phase 1: decide what the fund actually is

- [ ] Write the one-paragraph thesis. Draft: "Unc Fund writes the first small check into frontier-tech founders (blockchain, AI, software, robotics) before anyone else will. $500 to $9,999. Same-week decisions. Unc shows up."
- [x] Decide the vehicle. Decided 2026-09-05: a plain single-member LLC started personally, no partners at first, investing personal money via SAFEs. No outside LPs, so no fund admin, no Form D, no accredited-investor paperwork until that changes.
- [ ] Form the LLC. Delaware or home state (home state is cheaper and simpler for a solo LLC with no outside investors). Name "Unc Fund LLC". Registered agent, EIN, operating agreement. Stripe Atlas, Clerky, or the state site directly.
- [ ] One lawyer call before the first check: confirm the LLC can hold SAFEs, confirm nothing on the website reads as a public securities offering, and confirm what changes the day someone else wants to put money in. Reading for that day: https://sydecar.io/learn/who-can-be-a-vc, https://www.angellist.com/funds/venture-funds, https://www.allocations.com/blog/how-to-launch-a-micro-vc-fund-with-less-than-10m-(2026-guide)
- [ ] Decide check sizes and count. Draft: $500 (the "Unc Bucks" meme check), $2,500 standard, $9,999 max. Target 20 to 40 checks in year one.
- [ ] Decide the instrument: post-money SAFE (YC standard), uncapped with MFN for the smallest checks so nobody argues about a $500 valuation.
- [ ] Decide decision process: one form, one call, decision in 7 days. Publish it. That is the product.
- [ ] Define what Unc gives beyond money: intros, weekly office hours, a spot in the portfolio group chat, an Unc vest.
- [ ] Open a business bank account (Mercury or Brex) once the LLC exists.
- [ ] Set up Carta or Cap Table Pro (or a spreadsheet) for tracking SAFEs.

## What actually drives reach

**Freshness and reply ratio, not follower count.** The strongest performers were replies posted
within an hour to posts that had high views and few existing replies.

| Target | Their views | Replies when Unc arrived | Unc got |
|---|---|---|---|
| @paulg revenue graph | 85K | 66 | 234 |
| @himanshustwts AI deck | 4.8K | 1 | 228 |

Note the second row. A post with 5% of the reach delivered the same result, because Unc was reply
number two instead of number sixty-seven. **Chase the ratio, not the follower count.**

The inverse also holds. Replies to tiny posts return almost nothing: @msbathgate at 24 views
returned 2. Those are relationship plays with newly-followed investors, not reach plays, and should
be a small minority of the total.

  Worth following for the occasional human moment, not worth chasing.

  expired and posts now underperform replies by roughly 10x. **Replies are the growth engine;
  original posts are for the people who already arrived.**

## Honest assessment

Two days in, this is a normal early trajectory: 11 followers, a working content pipeline, 40 posts
scheduled through Sep 20, and reply reach that just jumped an order of magnitude. Nothing has gone
viral and nothing should be expected to yet.

The pieces that matter are in place: verified badge, discoverability unlocked, a real reply
doctrine, automated rounds three times a day, and a measurement habit that reports view counts back
each round instead of assuming.

**The gap is conversion.** Views are climbing but likes and profile clicks are not yet following.
228 views on a reply with one like means people are seeing Unc and scrolling past. That is the next
thing to solve, and it is a copy problem rather than a distribution problem.

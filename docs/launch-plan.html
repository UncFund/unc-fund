Due diligence on the competition

How VC firms build their websites and marketing

40 fund homepages were fetched and fingerprinted for their platform. Eleven of the playful or personality-first funds were read in full.

Six things the scan says

  1. Webflow is the default for small funds. Banana, Shrug, Weekend, Hustle Fund, Afore, Homebrew, Betaworks, Craft and Moxxie all run on it. Framer is the challenger: Sequoia, Uncork, 20VC.
  2. Big brands run WordPress or custom Next.js. a16z, Founders Fund, USV, Lightspeed, Mayfield and Pear are WordPress. First Round and Contrary are Next.js. That is content volume, not taste.
  3. Notion is the quiet backend. Shrug, First Round, Long Journey, Ludlow and Pear embed Notion for portfolio lists and applications. Cheap and fine at our size.
  4. Motion is table stakes at the top. Homebrew and Moxxie use GSAP scroll animation, Unusual uses Lottie. Rive is the tool of choice for interactive mascots, which is exactly what an animated Unc needs.
  5. Playful funds win on personality, not polish. Banana Capital's site is a one-page letter with emoji. Shrug's is 100+ testimonials and a merch shop. Hustle Fund has a hippo. It works because the brand lives on X, not the website.
  6. Media is the moat. Not Boring (newsletter), 20VC (podcast), Banana (The Peel podcast plus memes), Ludlow (a car-ride video show). The website is a landing page for an audience built elsewhere.

Identity

Brand: meet Unc

The uncle who doesn't fully get your startup but believes in you anyway, and he has a checkbook. This page is set in the proposed palette and type so you can judge it live.

The character

  • Who. Mid-50s, comfortable, "did well in the '90s". Slightly behind on slang and uses it wrong on purpose. Extremely warm. Writes small checks fast because life is short and so is his attention span.
  • Wears. Fleece vest zipped exactly halfway, small UNC patch where a firm logo would go. Light blue oxford, khakis, grey runners, readers pushed up on the forehead.
  • Shape. Big head, small body, so he reads at avatar size. Eyebrows do the acting. Grey temples. Mustache, after debate.
  • Style. Flat vector with hand-drawn line weight, not glossy 3D. A pixel-art variant for memes and Discord.
  • Rule. The joke is always on Unc or on VC culture, never on the founder. "Unc" comes from a term of respect. Keep it affectionate.

Animated version, built in Rive

  • idle: breathing, blinks, adjusts the vest
  • wave on page load, look follows the cursor
  • think: chin scratch while a form validates
  • approve: thumbs up on form submit
  • check: pulls a check from the vest and hands it forward

Ten poses to commission

Hands in pockets. Wave. Thumbs up. Writing a check with a huge pen. Holding a novelty check for $500. Squinting at a phone ("what is a zk rollup"). Arms crossed, nodding slowly. Pointing at the camera. Sitting in a car. Asleep at a demo day.

Voice

  • I don't understand your business. I like you. Here's $2,500.
  • Pre-seed? Son, this is pre-pre-seed. There's no seed yet. Just soil.
  • Rounds close fast. Unc closes faster.
  • Unc doesn't do 47-slide decks. Unc does a phone call.
  • If your grandfather could explain it, we're not interested. If your grandfather could fund it, we're not needed.

Every email signs off: "Proud of you. — Unc"

Type

  • A (this page, recommended): Fredoka for the wordmark and headings, Source Sans 3 for body, JetBrains Mono for check amounts. Friendly, a little dad-like.
  • B: Fraunces at display size plus JetBrains Mono. Reads like a family office that hired a designer.
  • C: Full pixel system with Press Start 2P for accents only. Only if 8-bit becomes the whole brand.

Logo and wordmark

  • Wordmark: UNC heavy, .FUND light, the dot drawn as a tiny zipper pull.
  • Avatar: Unc's face in a circle, eyebrows up, half smile.
  • Favicon and patch: a half-zipped vest silhouette in a rounded square.
  • Deal frame: a tiny cheque with UNC on the signature line.

Merch that is also marketing

  • The Unc Vest: blank fleece with an embroidered UNC patch. Patagonia stopped co-branding for finance firms, so patch a blank one or use another supplier.
  • "Backed by Unc" laptop sticker for founders.
  • Dad hat: UNC on the front, .FUND on the back.
  • One novelty-sized cardboard check, reused in every deal photo.
  • "Proud of you" mug for LPs.

Meme formats Unc owns

  • Unc reads a term sheet. Squinting at documents, caption is any confusing jargon.
  • Unc's take. A serious frontier-tech opinion as a quote card. This one builds credibility.
  • Deal announcement. Founder logo, Unc thumbs up, check amount shown proudly however small.
  • Pass announcement. "Unc passed. Unc will be wrong." Links to the anti-portfolio.

Website plan

Three visitors in priority order: a founder who wants to know if Unc will write a check, a friend or LP checking it is real, and someone who saw a meme. The site's job is the pitch form in under 30 seconds.

Stack

  • Build in code, not Webflow, because the mascot and the deck upload are the product. Next.js on Vercel, Cloudflare DNS, plain CSS with the brand tokens. The codebase lives in the repo under site/.
  • Mascot: animated inline SVG at launch (blinks, eyes follow the cursor, waves). Swap in a Rive state machine when the character sheet is final.
  • Content: Markdown in the repo. Notion sync later if non-coders need to edit.
  • Pitch form: native. Deck file uploads straight from the browser to Vercel Blob, then the fields plus the deck link are emailed to contactuncfund@gmail.com through Resend. A separate general contact form does the same without the pitch fields.
  • Analytics: Plausible or Vercel Analytics, so no cookie banner.
  • Fallback: Webflow plus a Rive embed still ships in a weekend if nobody wants to maintain code.

Home page, section by section

  1. Hero. Wordmark, "Small checks. Big uncle energy.", one-sentence thesis, "Pitch Unc" and "Follow @uncfund". Unc waves on load, eyes follow the cursor.
  2. Check slider. Unc's face and caption change at $500, $2,500, $5,000 and $9,999.
  3. What we fund. Four tiles: Blockchain, AI, Software, Robotics. One line of thesis and one Unc-ism each.
  4. How it works. Form in 5 minutes, call in 20, money in a week. No 47-slide decks, no warm intro required.
  5. Portfolio strip, then the latest notes, then the footer: socials, newsletter, "Proud of you. — Unc", disclaimer.

Pitch form fields

Name, X handle, email. Company and a 140-character one-liner. Category (Blockchain / AI / Software / Robotics / Unc won't get it but try). Stage. "What would you do with $2,500?" in 200 words. One link. Deck upload: PDF, PPTX or Keynote up to 25 MB, drag-and-drop. Checkbox: "I understand Unc's checks are small and his love is large."
